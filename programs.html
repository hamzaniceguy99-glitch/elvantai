The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 10 weeks, all worked on together, with practice between the sessions.

Program 01 · Be heard, actually

Everyday communication

The basics, done deliberately. We practice reflecting back what your partner said before responding, turning complaints into specific requests, and holding a short weekly conversation so problems surface while they're still small.

  • 8 weeks
  • Both partners
  • 1 hour a week together
  • Same call

Turning “you never” into a request that can actually be met.

Fifteen minutes, same time, short agenda.

Weeks 1–2 · The map

Writing the pattern out together, calmly, when it isn't happening.

Weeks 3–4 · The pause

An agreed way to stop before it escalates, that neither of you resents.

Weeks 5–6 · Repair

Coming back within 24 hours and what to say.

Weeks 7–8 · The underlying need

What the fight is usually standing in for.

Program 02 · Break the loop

The recurring fight

Recurring fights follow a script. We write yours down — the trigger, the first move, the escalation, the silence afterward — and design interruptions: an agreed pause, a signal, and a repair conversation that happens within a day rather than three days later.

  • 8 weeks
  • Both partners
  • 1 hour a week together
  • Willingness to look at it

Start this program

Program 03 · Fair, and settled

Money & chores

Most of this is a systems problem wearing an emotional coat. We make the invisible work visible, agree a split that both of you consider fair, and write down the decisions so they stop being renegotiated every week. For investment or tax decisions, see a licensed adviser.

  • 8 weeks
  • Both partners
  • 1 hour a week together
  • Both incomes

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · The full list

Every recurring task and bill, including the invisible ones.

Weeks 3–4 · Money

Shared and personal, proportional or equal, decided explicitly.

Weeks 5–6 · The mental load

Ownership of whole tasks rather than being reminded.

Weeks 7–8 · Review

A monthly fifteen-minute review that keeps it current.

Weeks 1–2 · Where it went

An honest look at how your time together is actually spent.

Weeks 3–4 · Rituals

Two small recurring things you'll both protect.

Weeks 5–6 · Attention

Noticing and saying so, as a practiced habit.

Program 04 · More than logistics

Time together

Long-term partnerships drift toward being a well-run operations meeting. We build small, repeatable rituals — a real conversation each week, something to look forward to each month — and practice attention and appreciation, which sound soft and change how a week feels.

  • 6 weeks
  • Both partners
  • 2 hours a week together
  • A shared calendar
